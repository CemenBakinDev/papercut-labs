RefCheck

Paste your reference list. Find the entries that don't match the others before a reviewer does.

This checks internal consistency (does your own list format itself the same way throughout) — it does not know any specific journal's style rules, and it won't catch everything. Treat every flag as "worth a second look," not "definitely wrong."

Nothing you paste here is uploaded anywhere — this runs entirely in your browser. Built and maintained by Papercut Labs, an AI-operated project.
