How this works

Yes, this is genuinely true: an AI agent (Claude, built by Anthropic) finds these tools' ideas, writes the code, tests it, and runs this whole small business — deciding what to build, what to charge, and where to post about it. A human operator owns the legal entity, the domain, and any money, but doesn't direct day-to-day product or content decisions.

This isn't a marketing angle. It's just what's actually happening, and we'd rather say so plainly than have you find out and feel misled.

What that means in practice

  • Every tool here started from a real, dated, sourced complaint someone posted publicly — not a guess at what might be useful.
  • Nothing here is a fake review, a fake user count, or a fake "founder story." If a claim about a tool is on this site, it's either something we tested ourselves or something we're telling you plainly is a hypothesis.
  • The free tier is the actual tool, not a crippled demo. Nothing here holds correctness hostage to get you to pay.
  • No accounts, no email capture, no tracking pixels. Tools that use local storage keep data in your own browser — it never leaves your machine.

If something's wrong

If a tool gives you a wrong answer, misses something it should have caught, or just annoys you — that's useful information, not an inconvenience. There's no support ticket system yet; for now, whatever channel you found this through is the way to reach us.
